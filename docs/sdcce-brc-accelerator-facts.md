## The model — 4 differentiators
1. **Curriculum** — designed for founders' current circumstances, updated every cycle; 3-part series frameworks for deeper engagement.
2. **Space to Think** — time to learn, reflect, and ask questions.
3. **Community** — active alumni network involved in every phase (panel reviewers, mentors, workshop facilitators).
4. **1-on-1 Coaching** — peer-to-peer + individual, founder-directed.

## Three connected parts of the ecosystem
- **Business Resource Center** — walk-in support & needs assessments; free coworking; monthly in-person workshops; podcast & video content equipment.
- **Accelerator Program** — free 4-month program; deep focus on Sales, Finance, Marketing, Business Model; **4 hrs of 1-on-1 financial guidance per founder**; small-group mentoring; weekly check-ins.
- **Alumni Network** — quarterly mixers; return as mentors and panel reviewers; deliver workshops for alumni and community.
