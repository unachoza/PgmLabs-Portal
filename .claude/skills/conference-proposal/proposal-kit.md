Many organizations are experimenting with AI, but few leaders have the opportunity to build something themselves. In partnership with AI Trailblazers, Program Labs helped field a Social Impact Hack-AI-thon that brings nonprofit executives, entrepreneurs, community leaders, and technologists together to solve real organizational challenges using AI. As the event's community anchor, Program Labs contributes the nonprofit infrastructure, alumni network, and local venue path that keep the experience inclusive and grounded in real community needs, while AI Trailblazers brings the hackathon platform and technical mentorship. Rather than focusing solely on coding or competition, participants identify a problem from their own work and leave with both a working solution and the confidence to continue building.

Using examples from the AI Trailblazers Hack-AI-thon — including the story of an executive director who built her first AI bot to address an organizational challenge — this session shares a practical framework for co-designing inclusive innovation experiences that strengthen communities, organizations, and regional ecosystems.

Participants will leave with strategies for creating hackathons that foster collaboration, accelerate learning, and translate AI experimentation into lasting organizational capacity.
