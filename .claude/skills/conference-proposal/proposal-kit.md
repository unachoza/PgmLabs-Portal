## 3. Signature talk

**Title:** From AI Consumers to AI Builders: Designing Hackathons That Build Community Innovation Capacity

**One-line hook (≤ ~120 chars):**
How Program Labs turns nonprofit leaders from AI consumers into AI builders — through hackathons that leave lasting capacity, not just prototypes.

**Learning objectives (attendees will leave able to):**
1. Apply a repeatable framework for designing inclusive AI hackathons that move participants from consuming AI to building with it.
2. Use facilitation tactics that help non-technical leaders build a working AI solution to a real organizational challenge in a single weekend.
3. Translate hackathon momentum into lasting organizational capacity and a connected regional innovation network.

**Session formats offered:** keynote · breakout/session · interactive workshop · panel
**Lengths available:** 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 min (workshop best at 60–90)
**Audience level:** all levels; designed for non-technical leaders
**Track fit:** nonprofit leadership · economic/workforce development · philanthropy/foundations · inclusive entrepreneurship · civic/community innovation

### 3a. Session description — LONG (verbatim, ~180 words)
Many organizations are experimenting with AI, but few leaders have the opportunity to build something themselves. Program Labs developed an AI hackathon model that brings together nonprofit executives, entrepreneurs, community leaders, and technologists to solve real organizational challenges using AI. Rather than focusing solely on coding or competition, participants identify a problem from their own work and leave with both a working solution and the confidence to continue building.

Using examples from the AI Trailblazer Hackathon — including the story of [ED-NAME], an executive director who built her first AI bot to address an organizational challenge — this session shares a practical framework for designing inclusive innovation experiences that strengthen communities, organizations, and regional ecosystems.

Participants will leave with strategies for creating hackathons that foster collaboration, accelerate learning, and translate AI experimentation into lasting organizational capacity.

### The framework — "The Program Labs AI Innovation Journey"
1. Convene diverse leaders
2. Surface real community challenges
3. Teach AI fundamentals
4. Build a working solution
5. Test and receive feedback
6. Leave with a new capability
7. Continue implementation through an innovation network
